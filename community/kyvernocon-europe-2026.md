# KyvernoCon Europe 2026

**Program committee member.** KyvernoCon Europe 2026, a co-located event at KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe. Amsterdam, March 2026.

A half-day gathering of the Kyverno community alongside KubeCon, for people running policy-as-code rather than evaluating it.

### What the work involved

Reviewing talk submissions and helping decide what made the programme. On a half-day event the constraint is sharp: a handful of slots, and every accepted talk displaces another.

What I brought to it was a narrow but real angle. I run Kyverno in a regulated environment, which makes some proposals easy to assess and others harder than they look. A policy that works in a demo and a policy that survives an audit are not the same artifact, and the difference rarely shows in an abstract.

### How it came about

Not through an application. I had been writing about Kyverno for a while, publishing policies, and arguing in the open for admission-time enforcement over after-the-fact reporting. The invitation followed from that.

Worth stating plainly, because it is the part that generalises: the committee seat was a consequence of the work being public, not a goal I set out to reach.
