# Cloud Native Days France

**Editorial team and selection committee.** Cloud Native France, the non-profit association behind the event. Since 2026.

CND France is the French-speaking cloud-native and DevOps conference, run entirely by volunteers from the communities in Paris, Lyon, Bordeaux, Aix, Marseille, Montpellier and Luxembourg. The 2026 edition brought 1,700 attendees and 40 partners to the CENTQUATRE in Paris for 60 talks. The next one is on **3 June 2027**, same venue.

### What the work involves

Two things, both of them about the programme rather than the event itself.

**Editorial.** Shaping what the conference is about: which subjects deserve a slot this year, where the gaps are, and how the day holds together as a whole rather than as sixty unrelated talks.

**Selection committee.** Reading talk submissions and deciding. This is the part with real weight: a submission is someone's work, and a decision either way deserves to be made on the substance rather than on the abstract's polish.

The association's stated line is experience reports over marketing. Applying that as a reviewer means preferring the talk that says what went wrong to the one that says what the product does.
