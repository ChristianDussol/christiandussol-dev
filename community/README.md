# Community

Where I serve rather than publish.

The writing on this site is mine to decide. The work on this page is not: it is time given to organisations that asked, and it comes with obligations to people other than me. Reviewing a talk proposal, reading someone else's architecture before they ship it, shaping a programme. **Different register, same constraint: say what you actually know, and say where you stop.**

This page lists the commitments. Each entry gives the role, the organisation, the period, and what the work involves. No adjectives.
